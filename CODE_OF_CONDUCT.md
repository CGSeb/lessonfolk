# Code of conduct

LessonFolk teaches AI to anyone, complete beginners included. The project should feel the same
way to the people who build it: patient, plain-spoken and welcoming.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many contributors are new to open source, to AI or to English.
- Explain rather than dismiss. "This breaks X because…" helps; "this is wrong" does not.
- Criticise the work, never the person.
- Accept that the maintainer may say no, and say no politely yourself.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's origin, gender, age, disability, religion,
  orientation or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their agreement.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainer ([@CGSeb](https://github.com/CGSeb))
privately. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may
block people who keep ignoring this code.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
